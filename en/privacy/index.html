Privacy Policy

This site takes only the minimum information needed to send the newsletter. What is collected, where it is used and when it is deleted is set out below.

In effect from 25 November 2026

1. What we collect

ItemWhenPurpose
Email addressOn subscribingSending the newsletter
Time of consent, page the request came fromOn subscribingRecording consent, managing delivery
Access log (IP, browser information)On visiting a pageHosting provider security and fault handling

We do not collect names, telephone numbers, addresses or payment details.

2. How it is used

  • Sending the newsletter (1 story at a time)
  • Confirming subscriptions, handling unsubscribe requests and delivery errors
  • Keeping a record of consent

It is not used for any other purpose, and no advertising information is sold or passed to a third party.

3. Retention and deletion

An email address is deleted without delay when the subscription is cancelled. A record left in a delivery system backup is deleted within at most 30 days. Where other law requires retention, the address is kept for that period.

4. Processing by others and third parties

No personal data processing is currently entrusted to an outside party. If a delivery service or mailing tool is introduced, the processor and the work entrusted will be listed on this page first.

Data is not provided to third parties except where an investigating authority makes a lawful request under statute.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies of its own and contains no visitor analytics or advertising scripts.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When they are requested, the IP address of the connecting device and similar data may be sent to Google. The font request is unrelated to the newsletter, and Google’s own policy applies.

The hosting provider may keep access logs to deliver the service and keep it secure.

6. Status of the subscription form

Subscriptions are being prepared. While requests are not being accepted, the screen says so, and the address you enter is not sent to a server. When the service opens, this page will say so.

7. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time.

  • To request access to the information collected
  • To request correction of an error
  • To request deletion and suspension of processing
  • To withdraw consent (cancel the subscription)

Send requests to hello@readersfiles.com. They are handled within 10 days of confirming your identity.

8. Children’s data

Subscriptions from children under 14 are not accepted. Where we learn of one, the address is deleted immediately.

9. Security measures

All traffic uses HTTPS, and the number of people with access to personal data and their permissions is kept to a minimum. Access rights to the delivery system are reviewed regularly.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date and the change are recorded on this page. A change unfavourable to subscribers is also announced in the newsletter.
