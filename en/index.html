How this record is written

Emotion is a conclusion drawn from verification, not the other way round.

Sources first

Words are checked against biographies, letters and newspapers of the time. Lines that cannot be verified are marked as ‘widely attributed’.

Image policy

Portraits are editorial illustrations only, and every image states that it is AI-generated and not a historical photograph.

Numbers in Arabic numerals

Ages, years, counts and frequencies are written as 67, 13 buildings, 10,000 people, so a reader can compare them at a glance.
