Editorial Principles

How this record is written

Emotion is a conclusion drawn from verification, not the other way round. That is why this magazine puts the hardest day at the centre, not the moment of success.

What we write

LEADERS FILES covers the ‘failed day’ of people whose lives can be checked against public-domain primary sources. Not the birth date and the list of achievements, but a day on which a decision had to be made, and the words left on that day.

Only historical figures long dead. Portraits of living people and press photographs still under copyright are not used.

Sources first

Dates and events rely first on primary sources (newspapers, letters and official records of the time), with secondary works used to cross-check. Every article ends with a ‘source note’ naming the material it rests on.

  • Statements are quoted directly only where the source is confirmed.
  • Lines that were polished later, or whose origin is unclear, are marked ‘widely attributed’.
  • Where figures differ between sources, the difference is not hidden: it is stated in the text or in the source note.

Numbers and dates

Ages, years, counts and frequencies are written in Arabic numerals: 67, 13 buildings, 10,000 people. A reader should be able to compare numbers at a glance.

Image policy

Every portrait on this site is an editorial portrait (AI-generated). None is a historical photograph, and none is presented in a way that could be mistaken for one. Under each image runs the line ‘Editorial portrait (AI-generated) · Not a historical photograph’.

Background and decorative images follow the same rule. Where a public-domain source image is used, its source and holding institution are named.

AI disclosure

Portrait images were produced with an image generation model. The text is written and checked by the editorial desk against the sources. No AI-generated sentence is published as fact without verification.

Corrections

If you find an error, write to hello@uncledison.com. Once a factual error is confirmed, the text is corrected and the date and content of the correction are recorded below the article. Purely stylistic edits are not separately marked.

Advertising and sponsorship

There is no advertising and no affiliate link on this site at present. If advertising is introduced, the advertising area will be clearly marked and kept separate from editorial content.
